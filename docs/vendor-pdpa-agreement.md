# Vendor PDPA Undertaking and Personal Data Processing Agreement - Elaborate Version

**Project:** FEN Learn LMS

**Effective Date:** [Insert date]

This Vendor PDPA Undertaking and Personal Data Processing Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between:

1. **Project Owner / Data Controller:** [Insert legal name of your company/organisation] ("Company")
2. **Vendor / Data Processor:** [Insert vendor legal name] ("Vendor")

Together, the Company and the Vendor are referred to as the "Parties".

## 1. Purpose

The Vendor is engaged to provide goods and/or services for the FEN Learn LMS project and may, in the course of that engagement, access, collect, store, use, disclose, transfer, or otherwise process personal data.

The purpose of this Agreement is to set out the Vendor's obligations when handling personal data in connection with the project and to ensure compliance with the Malaysian Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) and any applicable data protection requirements.

## 2. Definition of Personal Data

For the purpose of this Agreement, "personal data" means any information relating directly or indirectly to an identified or identifiable individual, including but not limited to:

- full name
- email address
- phone number
- identification number
- login credentials
- user profile information
- learning activity records
- assessment or progress data
- support correspondence
- any other data that can identify a user, staff member, learner, or administrator

## 3. Vendor Undertaking

The Vendor agrees that it shall:

1. Process personal data only on documented instructions from the Company, unless required otherwise by law.
2. Use personal data solely for the purpose of performing the services under the project.
3. Keep personal data confidential and restrict access to personnel who need it to perform the services.
4. Implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal data from loss, misuse, unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
5. Not disclose personal data to any third party without prior written approval from the Company, except where required by law.
6. Ensure that any subcontractor, sub-processor, employee, contractor, or agent with access to personal data is bound by equivalent confidentiality and data protection obligations.
7. Promptly notify the Company upon becoming aware of any actual or suspected personal data breach, security incident, unauthorised access, or data loss.
8. Assist the Company, where reasonably required, with requests relating to data access, correction, deletion, or complaint handling.
9. Return or securely delete personal data, and any copies of it, upon completion of the services or upon the Company's written request, unless retention is required by law.
10. Not transfer personal data outside Malaysia without the Company's prior written consent and appropriate legal safeguards.

## 4. Confidentiality

The Vendor shall keep all personal data and any related confidential information strictly confidential and shall not use it for any purpose other than the project.

This obligation continues during the term of the engagement and after it ends.

## 5. Security Requirements

Without limiting the Vendor's obligations, the Vendor shall:

- use strong access controls and unique user accounts where practical
- store personal data only in approved systems or locations
- encrypt sensitive data in transit and, where appropriate, at rest
- apply least-privilege access principles
- keep systems, devices, and software reasonably up to date
- maintain appropriate backup and recovery procedures
- promptly remove access when personnel no longer require it

## 6. Incident Notification

If the Vendor becomes aware of a suspected or actual personal data incident, the Vendor shall notify the Company without undue delay and provide, to the extent known:

- the nature of the incident
- the categories of data affected
- the approximate number of affected individuals
- the likely consequences
- the corrective actions taken or proposed

The Vendor shall cooperate with the Company in investigating and remediating the incident.

## 7. Audit and Compliance Support

Upon reasonable request, the Vendor shall provide information demonstrating compliance with this Agreement and with applicable PDPA obligations, subject to confidentiality and legal restrictions.

## 8. Ownership of Data

All personal data processed under this Agreement remains the property and responsibility of the Company or the relevant data subject, as applicable. The Vendor does not acquire any ownership rights in the personal data.

## 9. Retention and Deletion

The Vendor shall retain personal data only for as long as necessary to perform the services or as required by law. Once retention is no longer required, the Vendor shall securely delete, destroy, or anonymise the personal data, and confirm completion in writing if requested.

## 10. Breach of Agreement

Any breach of this Agreement may result in immediate suspension of access to personal data, termination of the engagement, and any other remedies available to the Company at law or in equity.

## 11. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Malaysia, and the Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Malaysia.

## 12. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties concerning the Vendor's handling of personal data for the project and may only be amended in writing signed by both Parties.
