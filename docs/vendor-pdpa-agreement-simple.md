# Vendor PDPA Undertaking - Simple Version

**Project:** FEN Learn LMS

**Effective Date:** [Insert date]

This agreement is for vendors or service providers working on the FEN Learn LMS project.

By signing, the Vendor agrees to follow the Malaysian Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) and to handle any personal data received from the Company responsibly and securely.

## Vendor Commitment

The Vendor agrees to:

- use personal data only for the project
- keep all personal data confidential
- protect personal data with reasonable security measures
- limit access to people who need it for the project
- not share personal data with others without written approval from the Company, unless required by law
- notify the Company quickly if there is any suspected data breach, loss, or unauthorised access
- delete or return personal data when the project ends or when the Company asks for it, unless the law says otherwise
- make sure any subcontractor or helper follows the same obligations

## Important Rules

1. The Vendor must not use personal data for any purpose outside the project.
2. The Vendor must not transfer personal data outside Malaysia without the Company's written approval.
3. The Vendor must cooperate with the Company if there is any privacy complaint, request, or incident.
4. The Vendor must keep personal data safe during and after the project.

## Breach

If the Vendor does not follow this agreement, the Company may suspend access, end the engagement, and take any legal action available.

## Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of Malaysia.
